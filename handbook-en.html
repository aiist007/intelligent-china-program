China is no longer just a market to enter or a supply base to manage. In electric vehicles, smart manufacturing, and AI plus robotics, China has become a living laboratory for industrial-scale innovation. This program goes beyond observing what Chinese companies do — it equips global executives with an analytical framework for understanding why China's innovation systems work, and how these lessons apply to their own organizations, markets, and industries.

Program Overview

Intelligent China is a 12-day executive immersion across three four-day modules in Beijing, Shenzhen, and Shanghai & Hangzhou. It combines CKGSB faculty-led methodology sessions, company visits, closed-door executive dialogues, and investor roundtables — designed to help global executives understand not just what China is building, but how and why it works.

This is not a conventional China study tour. It is a learning journey built around three sectors where China has developed systemic advantages — electric vehicles, smart manufacturing, and AI plus robotics — and an analytical framework for understanding why these advantages emerged. Each module opens with a CKGSB faculty session that establishes the methodology, followed by field visits that bring the framework to life.

The program is designed for global executives and is also relevant to leaders from emerging markets seeking to understand the industrial innovation models that China has pioneered.

Program Highlights

Three Pillars, Four Cities

The program is structured around the three pillars that underpin China's industrial excellence: advanced manufacturing, business model innovation, and the systematic logic of China's economic development. Each module addresses one pillar through a focused city lens — Beijing for policy and EV strategy, Shenzhen for hardware innovation and manufacturing systems, Shanghai & Hangzhou for AI, robotics, and platform intelligence.

Methodology First, Case Studies Second

Each module opens with a CKGSB faculty-led session that establishes an analytical framework — not just a topic overview. The field visits that follow are designed to test and deepen that framework, turning observations into transferable insight.

Closed-Door Access to Decision Makers

Engage directly with company executives, founders, and industry experts — including CKGSB's alumni network, which leads one-fifth of China's most valuable brands. The program prioritizes private, off-the-record exchanges over standard corporate hospitality.

China vs Global Benchmarking

Each module includes structured comparisons with global peers to avoid a China-only narrative and ensure the lessons are transferable.

A Capstone You Can Use at Home

Every participant develops a China Industrial Opportunity Memo — a structured document that answers: What can we learn? Where can we partner? What should we monitor? What risks should we manage? And what methodology can we apply to our own organization?

Module 1 · Beijing — EV and Intelligent Mobility Ecosystem

Module positioning. Beijing is where China's EV strategy is made — policy, regulation, capital allocation, and state-enterprise coordination converge here. This module helps participants understand how China's EV system works as a system, not just as a collection of car companies.

Core question: How has China built systemic EV advantage, and what methodology underlies it?

Faculty methodology session: - China's Way of Economic Development: The State-Market Interface and Industrial Policy as a Competitive Tool — How does China's approach to building industries differ from market-led models? What can be learned — and what cannot be transferred?

Module 2 · Shenzhen — Smart Manufacturing and Hardware Innovation

Module positioning. Shenzhen and the surrounding Greater Bay Area are where China's hardware innovation system is most visible — from concept design to global delivery. This module helps participants understand manufacturing not as a cost story, but as a capability story — and the methodology behind it.

Core question: How do Shenzhen and the GBA use manufacturing density, engineering talent, and supply-chain proximity to build globally competitive products?

Faculty methodology session: - Advanced Manufacturing as Strategic Asset: China's Hardware Innovation Methodology — Why has Shenzhen become the world's most integrated hardware innovation ecosystem? What are the structural conditions, and which are replicable?

Module 3 · Shanghai & Hangzhou — AI, Robotics, and Platform Intelligence

Module positioning. Module 3 spans two complementary cities. Shanghai anchors the module with China's most ambitious AI companies — foundation models, humanoid robotics, and AI compute. Hangzhou brings the platform-scale anchor (Alibaba Cloud, Tongyi Qianwen) and a deep robotics / smart-hardware cluster. The module explores not just what Chinese AI can do, but the methodology behind how it scales.

Core question: How are China's AI, robotics, and platform ecosystems moving from model capability to real business applications — and what makes some of these innovations "epochal"?

Faculty methodology session: - Epochal Innovations: What Makes Some Innovations Structural, Not Incremental — Drawing on Dean Xiang Bing's research on ground-breaking innovations, this session provides a framework for distinguishing transformative shifts from hype — and applies it to China's AI, EV, and smart-manufacturing ecosystems.

About CKGSB

CKGSB

Cheung Kong Graduate School of Business (CKGSB) is China's first faculty-governed, privately-funded business school. It has more than 50 world-class faculty members, most of whom were tenured at top global schools such as Michigan, Duke, Wharton, and Yale. CKGSB's influential student and alumni network represents the best, brightest, and most influential leaders in China, with more than half at the Chairman or CEO level.

CKGSB has nurtured 151 unicorn companies since its inception. It has a vision of developing a global ecosystem that nurtures the next generation of breakthrough innovation. More than 4,500 global executives have participated in CKGSB programs offered in English and other non-Chinese languages since 2009.

CKGSB Alumni lead one-fifth of China's most valuable brands.

CKGSB is positioning itself as an indispensable center for understanding global innovation, with China as its primary lens — connecting established multinationals, unicorns, startups, social enterprises, think tanks, and government leaders worldwide.

Capstone: The China Industrial Opportunity Memo

Each participant or company team develops an action memo throughout the program. The memo is updated after each module and presented at the end of Module 3.

Suggested structure

  1. My industry's China exposure and opportunity
  2. What I learned from China's AI, EV, and smart manufacturing ecosystems
  3. Potential partners and collaboration models (OEM, ODM, JVs, licensing, sourcing, investment)
  4. Investment or market-entry themes to monitor
  5. Risks, constraints, and misconceptions (policy, compliance, data, export control, IP, geopolitics, cultural)
  6. What methodology from this program can I apply to my own organization and market?
  7. 90-day action plan after returning home
